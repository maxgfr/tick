# Fonts

`plex-condensed-500.woff2`, `plex-condensed-600.woff2`,
`plex-condensed-700.woff2`, `plex-sans-400.woff2`, `plex-sans-500.woff2` —
latin subsets of **IBM Plex Sans Condensed** and **IBM Plex Sans**, served
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- Source: <https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans+Condensed>,
  <https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans>
- License: <https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/>

Self-hosted on purpose: tick loads nothing from any server, ever — the fonts
ship in the bundle and are precached by the service worker like every other
asset, so the offline PWA keeps its type.
